## Supplementary external check of nine PhilPapers publications

The review's second revision charts the nine publications recovered by the post hoc PhilPapers check separately from the formal 32-publication corpus. The original corpus, PRISMA flow, and original quantitative tables are retained. The original codebook is reproduced unchanged in the external workbook. The corresponding author manually checked and confirmed the charting decisions against the codebook (human-in-the-loop); no independent second coding or inter-rater reliability assessment was performed.

- [External charting workbook](external_check_round2.xlsx): all nine source records, original codebook and permitted values, source abstracts, evidence locators, coding rationale, comparative counts, and illustrative coding alternatives. The `Abstracts` sheet provides a readable view of the abstracts. Eight sources have published abstracts; Lopes's *Phenomenology as Proto-Computationalism* has no abstract in the publisher PDF or article page. Its absence is documented rather than replaced with a new summary.
- [CSV export](external_check_round2.csv): the complete `External_chart` sheet in UTF-8 for inspection and reuse.
- [External-check report](external_check_round2.md): procedure, source-level interpretations, mode comparison, and coding sensitivity.

Methodological import or design guidance occurs in 3/9 external publications (33.3%), compared with 7/32 formal-corpus publications (21.9%). These are separate descriptive results; the sets are not pooled, and influence-mode counts do not establish implementation or effectiveness. The check does not establish stability of the original quantitative distribution across sources.

The quoted abstracts remain the work of their respective authors and are attributed by bibliographic record, DOI, and PDF locator. Source PDFs are not included. The repository contains workflow documentation and the API specification, but no separate written protocol or prospective protocol registration.
